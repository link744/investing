Welcome

Sep 26, 2026

This blog covers the investing process — index funds, dollar-cost averaging, and the boring math that compounds. The thesis up front: the two things that matter are how long you stay in and how low your costs are. Everything else is commentary.

Format: every post is one infographic with the numbers up front, then a short plain-English read. No jargon, no hype, no advice you didn't ask for — just the data, what it says, and what I'd do with it.
