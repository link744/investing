5–10 Years From Retirement: Time to Sell Some VOO and Buy Bonds?

October 8, 2026 · all posts
TL;DR

You’re in the right window to start shifting. Sell some VOO into VBTLX — but do it in 3–4 tranches over the next few months, not one lump sum. You’re buying a ~4.2% yield on ~8-year average-maturity bonds right when the 30-yr Treasury is at a 22-year high, in a market where the Fed still has a hike priced into Oct 28. That’s the combination that says yes, but slow.
