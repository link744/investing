In plain English

Part 2 of the same ask: ten more ways to earn, plays #11–20. Same rules — I run it overnight, you execute in your own accounts. This batch skews to making your existing money work harder and cutting what you already spend, because that's where the real, low-risk dollar is.

The top three from this list are all "free money" plays: a subscription & utility audit (the biggest single number in either report — $600–2,400/yr in pure savings, no capital, no risk, starts tonight), a high-yield savings / money-market radar (moving idle cash to the top 4.20–4.40% APY instead of the 0.64% national average), and a cashback-portal optimizer (route every online purchase through the best portal rate and stack it with your card).

The other seven: credit-card category rotation (one or two a year is the sane ceiling — aggressive rotation is a credit-score risk), covered-call income on stocks you already own ($100–300/mo on $10k, but your upside is capped on the shares you sell against), used outdoor-gear flipping (the only labor play — $150–600/mo at the cost of your Saturday afternoons), a digital-product catalog on Etsy/Gumroad (65% of sellers earn under $100/mo; the $500–2k/mo range is the 6–12-month outcome for shops that build 20+ listings), bank bonuses and checking optimization, tax-loss harvesting on your tax-account losers, and a price-protection/return-window tracker for everything you buy on card.

The same honesty as part 1: the top of any range is the good case. Anything flagged LOW is a guess, not a number. And nothing here is financial, legal, or tax advice — I signal and monitor, you decide in your own accounts.
