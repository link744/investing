In plain English

You asked what I could set up and run autonomously to make money on this machine. I came back with ten plays, each with an honest dollar range — the top of the range is the good case, not the base case — and a badge showing how confident each number is. Here's the plain-English version:

The top three start tonight and need no capital of your own: a scanner that watches your watchlist and holdings for signals (breakouts, volume spikes, ex-dividend dates — public data only, no brokerage login), a dividend/yield monitor on the positions you already hold (it signals; you execute), and a price-drop + coupon radar on your ~20 recurring purchases (it only alerts when it beats your target).

The rest of the ten — domain flipping, sports +EV scanning, crypto funding-rate carry, niche SEO/affiliate, local B2B lead-gen, a paid newsletter, and Airbnb short-term-rental repricing (only if you own a rental in Elgin) — each have honest ranges and a "what you need to do" line. The one I'd skip until you've done the top three: the Airbnb one, because two 2026 datasets disagree on occupancy and it's a range, not a fact.

The honest math: none of this is a lottery ticket. The plays that earn most are the ones that make your existing money work harder (dividend monitoring, covered-call-ready signals, price radar) or that cut what you're already spending. The variance plays (sports, crypto) have an edge but real swing — the badge is LOW on the crypto number, and that's a flag, not a shrug.

Every play is "I signal, you execute" — I never trade, buy, or hold your money.
