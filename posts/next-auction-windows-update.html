What this means for you

If your goal is a Treasury with duration under 3 years, the whole set narrows to one instrument: the 2-year note. Its window is Monday, Oct 26 — bid on TreasuryDirect from $100 or at a Vanguard brokerage account from $1,000, non-competitive, funded on the issue date Nov 2. The 5-yr and 10-yr TIPS windows are real opportunities for TIPS exposure, but they are 4.73y and 8.80y instruments — above the 3-year line, so they belong to a different goal (inflation protection), not a short-duration one.

  4. Evidence-based, not a guarantee. Dates can shift if Treasury moves the schedule — the 2-yr note's official announcement (Oct 22) and the 10-yr TIPS's (Nov 12) are the confirmation dates. This is a report on the auction calendar, not a forecast: the auction yield is whatever the auction sets.
Honest caveats

Durations use approximate yields (see assumption 2); the flag is robust to that error, but the exact duration numbers will tick as yields move. The 2-yr note's offering size and close times are not yet published — they land in its official offering announcement due Oct 22. TIPS duration here is measured in the real (inflation-adjusted) yield, which is the correct basis for a TIPS; a TIPS also varies with the CPI index, which is what its real duration is meant to capture — so for a true calendar-under-3-years horizon, the 2-yr note is the fit, and the TIPS windows belong to the inflation-protection goal instead. This is a how-to/calendar report, not a recommendation to buy any specific security.
