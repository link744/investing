Bond Tent Calculator

Short version: two numbers in — your age today and your planned retirement age — and out comes your recommended bond at every age from today to 90: the long-run band, the ramp up to the 55% bond-tent peak at retirement, and the taper back down. Part 3 of the bond-percent series: Part 1 found the band, Part 2 turned it into a table, and this tool lets you plug in your own two numbers.

Where this fits

How the numbers work

Two layers, same as Parts 1 and 2:

  1. Long-run band (30–50%). Set by your runway to age 85: the shorter your remaining runway, the more bonds — 30% with 30+ years left, stepping up five points per five-year step, capped at 50%.
  2. The bond tent. In the sequence-risk red zone — about five years before to five years after your planned retirement date — the allocation ramps to a 55% peak at the retirement date, then tapers back to the band.
Assumptions

1. Data window: 47 annual total-return pairs, 1979–2025, US-only (S&P 500 TR + 10-yr US Treasury TR). 2. The bond asset is the 10-yr Treasury total return — a duration measure, not a retail bond fund. 3. Horizon at retirement = 85 − retirement age (a planning anchor, not a guarantee). 4. Constant allocation, no withdrawals; no taxes, fees, or income modeling. 5. The 2026 YTD figures are estimates and are not in the sample. 6. The band is evidence-based, not a guarantee — it reflects 47 years of data, not all possible futures. 7. The table does not model the separate 5–10% cash/T-bill income bucket, which is additive to the bond allocation, not a substitute for it.

In plain English

Your bond today is set by how much runway you have to 85. But your bond % is not a flat line — it’s a tent. In the ~10 years straddling your planned retirement date, you climb to a 55% peak because that’s when sequence risk is worst (a bad market right at or just after retirement is the one that can’t be made up), then you taper back toward the band as the danger zone passes. The table above is that tent, drawn age by age for your two numbers. The chart shows the same thing as a curve: flat band, tent peak at your retirement age, flat band.

Method and data: Part 1 — 47 annual S&P 500 TR / 10-yr T-bond TR pairs (1979–2025), 200,000-path bootstrap Monte Carlo, seed 42, constant allocation, no withdrawals. Bond-tent framework: Kitces / Pfau. This is a self-contained tool (no external assets, no data requests) and runs entirely in your browser.
