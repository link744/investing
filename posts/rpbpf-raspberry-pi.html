RPBPF (Raspberry Pi): When to Buy Now

September 29, 2026 · all posts
TL;DR

Raspberry Pi is a genuinely strong business — roughly 46% of market cap in cash, growing revenue at 20–27% — but it is trading at a premium (~9.1x sales, ~47x earnings) after a sharp earnings-driven pop that has now fully retraced. It sits right at the $8.30 limit, not below it. The 5-level signal is WAIT (the middle rung): not a sell, not a buy — a limit order at $8.30 that may never fill while it holds above it. Consensus target $11.26 (~34% up).

Not investment advice. Figures are as of the Sep 29, 2026 close: RPI.L 645.50p on the LSE, RPBPF ADR $8.45, sourced from public market data (Yahoo Finance) and the Sep 24, 2026 research report. ADR figures are approximate USD equivalents of the London ordinary-share price.
