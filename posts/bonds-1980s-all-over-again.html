Is This the 1980s All Over Again? Bonds, 1979–2026

September 14, 2026 · all posts
In plain English

The "1980s all over again" take is a reflex, so I pulled the actual Fed numbers. The direction matches — 10-year climbing, inflation over 3%, Fed tightening — but the scale doesn’t: 1981 had 14.6% inflation and a 22%+ funds rate; we’ve got ~3% and 3.6%. And here’s the part everyone skips: 1981 was a ceiling, not a trajectory. The 10-year fell from 15.8% to ~10.5% by 1982, then climbed back up, and only went down for good after the ’90–91 recession. Translation: if inflation holds above 3%, expect the 10-year to push toward 5.5–6% before it peaks; if a recession breaks it, it drops below 4% and stays down for a decade.
