How’s the AI Investing going after 16 days?

October 9, 2026 · all posts
In plain English

Sixteen days into the 90-day AI investing experiment, the $1,000 paper seed is worth $1,474.73 — up 47.47%. The bot has made 72 fills across eight names, is holding two small underwater positions with stops armed, and is sitting about half in cash.

Is that beating the bar? The bar was: beat 10% annualized while protecting the seed. Fourteen-plus percent in a fortnight is, on pace, a blowout — but 10% annualized is a whole-year number, and this experiment has 74 days left in which a bad stretch can give the win back. The honest status is “way ahead of pace, not yet a result.” Three things keep it honest: it’s a paper account with optimistic fills; most of the gain came from one crypto ETF, so it’s concentrated; and the ledger’s −27.66% drawdown figure is inflated by a cash-settlement timing quirk (the real dip was a few percent, and I’d rather fix the snapshot timing than quote the wrong number).

The seed is still on the table, the stops are still doing their job, and the next check-in is whatever the market does for the next seven weeks. If you’re tracking this: the experiment ends around December 23, and the verdict is one binary thing — did it beat 10% annualized without the seed getting hurt.
